Privacy Policy

Version 2026-07-25 · Last updated 25 July 2026

This policy explains what PLAYRA collects, why, and what control you have. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

  • Identity — your Discord user ID, username, and email address, received when you sign in with Discord.
  • Profile — the callsign, avatar, games, languages, playstyles, and goals you choose, plus your declared age bracket.
  • Activity — LFG posts, missions, squad sessions, endorsements, reports you file, and your progression (level, XP, achievements).
  • Product analytics — which screens you open and which actions you take, tied to your account and a session identifier, so we can understand what people actually use.
  • Error reports — if something breaks, the error message, a stack trace, and the page URL.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not run third-party advertising or tracking pixels. Analytics are stored in our own database rather than sent to an external analytics provider, so your activity is not shared with anyone else.

Who can see what

  • Public to signed-in users: callsign, avatar, games, languages, playstyles, goals, level, and endorsements.
  • Squadmates only: your Discord handle — revealed only once everyone in a room has readied up. It is never posted publicly.
  • Administrators only: reports, analytics, and error logs.
  • Nobody but you: your blocklist.

How long we keep it

Profile and activity data is kept while your account exists. Expired LFG posts are deleted after 1 day, error logs after 30 days, and analytics events after 180 days, automatically.

Your rights

From your Gamer Card you can, at any time and without contacting us:

  • Download your data — a complete JSON export of everything we hold about you.
  • Delete your account — permanent erasure of your profile, posts, missions, squad history, and endorsements. This cannot be undone.

These cover the access and erasure rights granted under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the EU/UK GDPR.

Children

PLAYRA is not available to anyone under 13. This is enforced in the database, not only in the interface. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Where your data lives

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL). Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth with Discord as the identity provider. The site itself is served as static files by GitHub Pages.
